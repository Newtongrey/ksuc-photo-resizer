How cropping works

Auto-crop analyses visible page pixels, including scanned content. A small safety margin is added to reduce the risk of clipping text or page numbers. Each page is detected independently. Very faint content, white-on-white elements, unusual backgrounds, or isolated marks may affect detection, so review the result before sharing. Cropping changes the visible page area; it does not permanently erase content outside the crop boundary.
