Important PDF editing limitations

This editor adds text and annotations over the original page; it does not reliably rewrite the PDF's original text objects. “Cover & replace” places a white cover over visible content and adds replacement text, but it is not secure redaction and the covered original content may remain recoverable. Scanned PDFs are displayed as pages but their text is not editable without OCR. Signature drawing and annotations are not a verified digital signature. Some complex, encrypted, malformed or unusually large PDFs may fail or perform slowly.
